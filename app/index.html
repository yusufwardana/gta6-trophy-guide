Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no difficulty setting, no online trophies. The story takes about 60 to 80 hours; the whole platinum takes around 200 hours, most of it in the 100% cleanup. Cheats disable trophies, so keep them off your platinum save. Missions cannot be replayed, and there are no mission medals — but free roam continues after the story, and nothing on the list is missable except, possibly, one meditation in Chapter 1.

  1. Step 1 — Story and optional missions about 60–80 hours

    Play the campaign at your own pace. Along the way: do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house the morning Lucia walks out to the balcony (the one possible missable); take optional missions as they appear — the 100% checklist needs any 100 of them, out of several hundred; scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app (100% needs 200 of 277 base models); study new animals through the binoculars (100% needs 100 of 255); turn on progress and criminal profile notifications in the UI settings so you can see what counts.

    Yields: all 12 story trophies, the 5 side-character final missions as they open, and most of the one-off stunts.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    The checklist appears after the story under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Progress made during the story already counts. It is lenient — roughly 60% true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditation spots and 300 stunts, while robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests need everything. This step is where A Long Road, the gold 100% trophy, pops — and the platinum follows right after.

    Yields: A Long Road (gold) and nearly every activity, collectible and robbery trophy.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    Finish the 8 stranger mission strands — they are not part of the 100% checklist, so they may still be open. Then sweep whatever is left on the list: the last card sets, the last bank vaults, the last races. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops the moment the final trophy does.

    Yields: Strangers & Freaks (silver) and the platinum.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude. During Chapter 1, one morning at Jason's house, Lucia says she is going to meditate and walks out to the balcony. Follow her and accept the optional quest. That meditation may count toward the total even though it is not marked as a spot, and it is gone after Chapter 1. The other 18 meditation spots are marked and can be visited any time. No other trophy is known to be missable: story trophies unlock automatically, quest givers who leave during the story all return, and the 100% checklist is fully available after the story.

Save advice

The game has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave. Because missions cannot be replayed, keep a save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotate the rest during free roam.

What counts toward 100%

Story missions, robberies, gang operations, collectibles, races, stunts, challenges and discoveries. The exact per-category counts are on the 100% checklist in the game; this guide's trophy pages carry the counts the guide writers have confirmed.
